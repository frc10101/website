About Critical Hit

Our mission is to empower future STEM leaders in Alabama through the FIRST suite of programs. We are FRC Team 10101 - Critical Hit, a 2nd year team from Huntsville, Alabama, founded in 2025.

Meet Our Robot

Waka Waka

This is our current season robot. It represents our 2026 build focused on consistency, speed, and reliable game piece handling.

WoWoWoWo - Drive Base

Designed for quick movement and strong defense across the field.

Clyde - Launcher

Our 3-column launcher uses 3 motors to launch 3 FUEL at once.

PacMan - Intake

Our 21" intake uses grip rollers on an over the bumer system to intake FUEL from the ground.

Whoops Y Daisy

Meet our 2025 competition robot, designed around a brand-new funnel stage that kept game pieces flowing to the scorer.

Whoops - Our Swerve Drivetrain

Swerve drive for maximum maneuverability

Y - Our Funnel

Guides game pieces into Daisy, staging them for consistent scoring runs

Daisy - Our End Effector

Scores game pieces and uses a beam break to detect when one is loaded

Our Achievements

Rookie All-Star Award

Celebrates the rookie team exemplifying a strong partnership effort and the mission of FIRST to inspire students to learn more about science and technology.

2025

Rising All-Star Award

Celebrates the team that has persisted through challenges, despite the difficulties of being young. This could be the result of being a new team, or a team with recent turnover in membership.

2026

Engineering Inspiration Award

Celebrates a team who demonstrates outstanding success in advancing respect and appreciation for engineering within a team's school, organization, and community.

2026

Gracious Professionalism Award

Celebrates outstanding demonstration of FIRST Core Values such as continuous Gracious Professionalism, sportsmanship, and working together both on and off the playing field.

2026

FIRST Leadership Award

Recognizes outstanding passionate student leaders who demonstrate exemplary effectiveness at attaining FIRST ideals.

Our Sponsors

We’re grateful for the organizations that power Critical Hit Robotics and help us inspire future STEM leaders.

Boeing

Boeing is a leading global aerospace company and top U.S. exporter that develops, manufactures, and services commercial airplanes, defense products, and space systems for customers in more than 150 countries.

DoDSTEM

Argosy Foundation

PPG

GLOVIA
